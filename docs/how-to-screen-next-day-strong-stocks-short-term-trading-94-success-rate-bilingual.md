## 方法一：竞价量能筛选法，看开盘前的"资金态度"  
## Method 1: Pre-Market Volume Screening — Reading "Capital Attitude" Before the Open

**竞价阶段（9:15-9:25）是资金提前布局的窗口，量能是资金态度最直接的体现，这一方法核心就是通过竞价量比和价格表现，判断个股次日是否有资金主动拉升的意愿，实操性极强。**  
**The pre-market auction phase (9:15–9:25) is the window for capital to position early. Volume is the most direct reflection of capital intent. This method uses pre-market volume ratio and price action to judge whether capital has the active intent to push the stock higher the next day — highly actionable.**

**首先明确核心指标：量比，代表竞价阶段成交量相对近期平均量的倍数，短线筛选次日强势股，竞价量比建议看5倍以上，且数值稳定不跳水（9:20后竞价不可撤单，此时量比更真实）。**  
**First, clarify the core metric: Volume Ratio (量比) — the multiple of pre-market volume vs. recent average volume. For screening next-day strong stocks, require pre-market volume ratio ≥ 5x, and the value must be stable without plunging (after 9:20, auction orders cannot be cancelled, so the ratio is more authentic).**

**价格方面，优选平开或小幅高开（0.5%-3%）的个股，大幅高开（5%以上）容易出现资金兑现，低开则说明资金认可度低，都不是最优选择。**  
**On price: prefer flat open or slight gap-up (0.5%–3%). Large gap-ups (≥5%) invite profit-taking; gap-downs signal low capital conviction. Neither is optimal.**

**实操步骤很简单：9:20后打开行情软件，按竞价量比排序，筛选出量比5倍以上、高开0.5%-3%的个股；再看个股所属板块，若板块内多只个股同步出现这种竞价表现，说明板块有资金联动，个股走强的概率会翻倍；最后排除近期大幅上涨、处于高位的个股，高位股竞价量能再大，也大概率是资金借势出货。**  
**Execution is simple: after 9:20, open your terminal, sort by pre-market volume ratio, filter for ratio ≥5x and gap-up 0.5%–3%. Then check the sector — if multiple stocks in the sector show this pattern simultaneously, it indicates sector-wide capital coordination, doubling the odds of strength. Finally, exclude recent big gainers / high-position stocks — even with huge pre-market volume, high-position stocks are likely distribution traps.**

**这种方法的关键是"顺势"，跟着竞价阶段的真实资金走，资金愿意在开盘前花真金白银买入，次日拉升的动力自然更足，是筛选次日强势股最基础也最有效的方法。**  
**The key is "following the trend" — follow the real capital in the pre-market. If capital is willing to spend real money before the open, the momentum to push higher the next day is naturally stronger. This is the most fundamental and effective method for screening next-day strong stocks.**

## 方法二：尾盘异动跟随法，抓尾盘资金的"提前布局"  
## Method 2: Tail-End Anomaly Tracking — Catching Capital's "Early Positioning" at the Close

**尾盘（14:30-15:00）是多空双方最后博弈的阶段，不少主力会选择在尾盘悄悄布局，避免盘中拉升引起关注，尾盘异动的个股，往往是主力为次日拉升做的准备，这一方法就是捕捉这类"有备而来"的标的。**  
**The tail-end (14:30–15:00) is the final battleground between bulls and bears. Many main forces choose to position quietly at the close to avoid drawing attention during the session. Stocks with tail-end anomalies are often main forces preparing for the next day's push — this method captures such "well-prepared" targets.**

**尾盘异动的核心判断标准有两个：价格异动和量能异动，二者缺一不可。**  
**Two core criteria for tail-end anomalies — both must be present: price anomaly and volume anomaly.**

**价格上，个股尾盘出现稳步抬升，最后10分钟涨幅在1%-2%，且股价不回踩，走势很稳；**  
**Price: stock rises steadily in the tail-end, gaining 1%–2% in the last 10 minutes, without pullbacks — very stable action.**

**量能上，尾盘成交量明显放大，形成"尾盘放量上涨"的形态，且放量是主动性买盘居多，不是散户跟风成交。**  
**Volume: tail-end volume expands notably, forming a "tail-end volume surge up" pattern, and the surge is dominated by aggressive buy orders, not retail chasing.**

**同时要注意筛选条件：尾盘异动的个股，必须是处于低位或箱体震荡末端的，高位股尾盘放量很可能是主力出货；其次，个股基本面无利空，排除业绩暴雷、减持等负面消息，避免踩雷；最后，看均线支撑，5日均线、10日均线向上，股价站在均线上方，尾盘异动后次日拉升的支撑更足。**  
**Screening conditions also matter: the stock must be at a low level or the end of a boxing range — high-position stocks with tail-end volume surges are likely distribution. Next, no fundamental negatives — exclude earnings bombs, major sell-downs, etc., to avoid landmines. Finally, check MA support: 5-day and 10-day MAs trending up, price above MAs — tail-end anomaly then has stronger support for next-day surge.**

**尾盘布局的资金，目的就是在次日获取收益，只要不是高位出货，这类个股次日高开或冲高的概率极高，适合短线投资者尾盘选股，次日择机卖出。**  
**Capital positioning at the close aims to profit the next day. As long as it's not high-position distribution, such stocks have extremely high probability of gapping up or surging the next day — suitable for short-term traders to pick at the close and sell opportunistically the next day.**

## 方法三：板块龙头联动法，跟紧主线的"核心标的"  
## Method 3: Sector Leader Coordination — Sticking to the "Core Targets" of the Main Theme

**A股市场中，个股的强势往往离不开板块的支撑，主线板块的龙头股，永远是短线强势股的核心，板块龙头联动法，就是通过找主线板块，再选板块内的龙头标的，把握板块上涨带来的个股强势机会，这一方法的胜率远高于选独立行情的个股。**  
**In the A-share market, individual stock strength rarely exists without sector support. The leaders of main-theme sectors are always the core of short-term strong stocks. The Sector Leader Coordination method: find the main-theme sector, then pick the sector's leader(s) to capture the strong-stock opportunities driven by sector rallies. This method's win rate far exceeds picking isolated stocks.**

**第一步先找主线板块：看当日涨幅榜、资金流向榜，资金净流入靠前、涨幅榜前列的板块，且板块内涨停个股数量多、联动性强，就是当日的主线板块，主线板块的热度通常能延续到次日，这是个股走强的大前提。**  
**Step 1: Find the main-theme sector. Check daily gainers list and capital flow rankings — sectors with top net inflows and top gains, with many limit-up stocks and strong coordination, are the day's main theme. Main-theme heat typically carries into the next day — the prerequisite for individual stock strength.**

**第二步选板块龙头：主线板块里的龙头分两种，连板龙头和换手龙头，连板龙头是板块内涨停板数量最多的个股，资金辨识度最高，次日继续涨停的概率大；换手龙头是板块内成交量最大、资金承接最好的个股，即使没有连板，次日也会有冲高机会，适合不同风险偏好的投资者。**  
**Step 2: Pick the sector leader. Two types in the main theme: consecutive-limit-up leader (连板龙头) and turnover leader (换手龙头). The consecutive leader has the most limit-ups in the sector — highest capital recognition, high probability of another limit-up. The turnover leader has the largest volume and best capital absorption — even without consecutive limit-ups, it gets next-day surge opportunities, suiting different risk appetites.**

**第三步筛选次日强势股：确认主线板块后，在板块内选龙头标的，若龙头股当日缩量涨停，次日大概率高开继续走强；若龙头股当日换手涨停，成交量稳定，次日也会有资金继续拉升。同时排除板块内的跟风股，跟风股涨得慢、跌得快，不如龙头股强势。**  
**Step 3: Screen next-day strong stocks. After confirming the main theme, pick the leader(s) in the sector. If the leader hits limit-up on shrinking volume, next day likely gaps up and continues strong. If the leader hits limit-up with turnover but stable volume, capital will keep pushing next day. Meanwhile, exclude follower stocks in the sector — followers rise slow, fall fast, never as strong as the leader.**

**这种方法的核心是"跟主线、抓龙头"，主线板块的资金热度会形成合力，龙头股作为板块的核心，自然是次日最强势的标的，是短线操作中最稳妥的方法。**  
**The core is "follow the main theme, catch the leader." Main-theme capital heat creates collective force; the leader as the sector's core is naturally the next day's strongest target — the most reliable method in short-term trading.**
