## 概述 | Overview

**涨停后杀出一根巨量阴线，次日又被中大阳反包，这种走势到底是主力洗盘蓄力，还是拉高出货的陷阱？**  
**After a limit-up (涨停), a huge volume bearish candle (巨量阴线) appears, followed by a bullish reversal candle (反包阳) the next day. Is this a main force wash-out (洗盘) to accumulate strength, or a trap for distribution (出货)?**

**很多散户遇到这种情况就慌了神，要么早早卖出错失暴涨行情，要么盲目进场惨遭深套。**  
**Many retail investors panic in this situation — either selling too early and missing the rally, or blindly entering and getting trapped.**

**其实，只要掌握核心要点，就能精准识别主力意图，稳稳捕捉强势股的主升浪。**  
**By mastering the core criteria, you can precisely identify the main force's intent and steadily capture the main uptrend (主升浪) of strong stocks.**

## 形态解析："涨停+巨量阴+反包阳" | Pattern Analysis: "Limit-Up + Huge Volume Bearish + Reversal Bullish"

**这种"涨停+巨量阴+反包阳"的组合形态，大多出现在股价低位区域，是主力典型的强势洗盘手法。**  
**This combination pattern typically appears in low-price areas (低位区域) and is a classic strong wash-out technique by the main force.**

**当个股在低位横盘震荡一段时间后，拉出首板或二板涨停，说明多方力量开始发力，吸引了市场关注。**  
**After a stock has consolidated sideways at a low level for some time, it breaks out with a first or second limit-up, signaling that bullish forces are emerging and attracting market attention.**

**紧接着次日股价高开低走，收出一根放量阴线，成交量至少是前一交易日的两倍以上，这看似是上涨趋势被打断，实则是主力通过大幅震荡，吓出短线获利筹码和不坚定的跟风盘，完成筹码的快速交换。**  
**The next day, the stock opens high but sells off, closing with a huge volume bearish candle — volume at least 2x the limit-up day's volume. This appears to break the uptrend, but in reality, the main force uses violent oscillation to scare out short-term profit-takers and weak followers, completing a rapid chip exchange (筹码快速交换).**

## 四大核心要点：确认洗盘而非出货 | Four Core Criteria to Confirm Wash-Out (Not Distribution)

**想要确认这种形态是洗盘而非出货，必须紧盯四个核心要点，缺一不可。**  
**To confirm this pattern is a wash-out rather than distribution, you must monitor four core criteria — none can be missing.**

### 第一，看涨停板数与成交量 | 1. Limit-Up Count & Volume

**涨停板可以是首板，也可以是二板，关键是次日的阴线必须放出巨量，成交量要达到涨停当日成交量的两倍及以上。**  
**The limit-up can be the first board (首板) or second board (二板). Critical: the next day's bearish candle must have huge volume ≥ 2x the limit-up day's volume.**

**只有足够大的换手，才能说明主力完成了筹码清洗，为后续拉升扫清障碍。**  
**Only sufficient turnover confirms the main force has cleaned out chips, clearing obstacles for the next leg up.**

### 第二，看回调的支撑位 | 2. Pullback Support Level

**股价在出现巨量阴线后的短期回调过程中，绝对不能跌破前期涨停板的最低价；如果是跳空涨停，就以前一交易日的收盘价为支撑底线。**  
**During the short-term pullback after the huge volume bearish candle, price must not break below the limit-up day's low. If it was a gap-up limit-up, use the previous day's close as the support floor.**

**这个位置是主力的成本区间，一旦跌破，说明主力护盘意愿不强，大概率是出货走势。**  
**This is the main force's cost zone. A break below signals weak defense — likely distribution.**

### 第三，看反包的时间窗口 | 3. Reversal Time Window

**股价必须在3~5个交易日内，重新收回巨量阴线的收盘价上方。**  
**Price must reclaim above the huge volume bearish candle's close within 3–5 trading days.**

**这个时间窗口很关键，短时间内的快速反包，说明多方力量强势，主力不想给散户太多低位进场的机会；若超过5个交易日仍未收回，那么洗盘的可能性就会大大降低。**  
**This time window is critical. A quick reversal shows strong bullish force; the main force doesn't want to give retail too much time to enter low. If price fails to reclaim after 5 days, wash-out probability drops sharply.**

### 第四，看突破的入场点位 | 4. Entry Trigger: Break Above Bearish Candle's Open

**最稳妥的上车时机，不是在反包阳出现的当天，而是等股价突破巨量阴线的开盘价时。**  
**Safest entry: not on the reversal bullish candle day, but when price breaks above the huge volume bearish candle's OPEN price.**

**这个价位是前期的压力位，一旦突破，就意味着主力彻底掌控盘面，后续拉升的概率极高。**  
**This level is the prior resistance. A breakout means the main force fully controls the board — high probability of continued rally.**

## 实战案例 | Real-World Examples

**从实战案例来看，低位横盘后的首板涨停，次日收出巨量阴线，三日内股价就突破阴线最高价，进场后直接迎来一波主升浪；**  
**Case 1: Low-level consolidation → first limit-up → next day huge volume bearish → within 3 days price breaks above bearish candle's high → entry captured the main uptrend.**

**还有的二板涨停后出现巨量阴，反包阳出现后短暂调整，随后突破反包阳高点，同样开启了暴涨行情。**  
**Case 2: Second limit-up → huge volume bearish → reversal bullish appears → brief consolidation → break above reversal candle's high → explosive rally followed.**

**反之，如果这种形态出现在股价高位区域，且巨量阴线出现后股价跌破支撑位，迟迟无法反包，那么大概率是主力拉高出货的信号。**  
**Conversely, if this pattern appears in a high-price area (高位区域), and the huge volume bearish candle breaks support with no reversal within the time window — it is likely a distribution trap (拉高出货信号).**
